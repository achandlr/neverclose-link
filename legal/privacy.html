Never Close: Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Never Close is a personal app that lets two people keep a running tab of who owes whom. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

Your name, your mobile phone number, and the expenses and tabs you create. We use your phone number to verify your identity when you sign in and to connect you with friends you invite.

Text messages (SMS)

If you provide your mobile number, we send transactional text messages: one-time verification codes when you sign in, and invitation links when a friend invites you to a shared tab. Message frequency is occasional and transactional. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP.

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. Your number is used only to operate Never Close: verification and connecting you with people you choose to invite. Mobile information is never shared for marketing or promotional purposes.

How we handle your data

Your tabs and expenses are visible only to you and the one other person on each tab. Never Close never moves money and never connects to your bank or cards. If you allow contacts access, contacts are matched using irreversible hashes and are never uploaded.

Your choices

Delete your account anytime in the app (Settings → Delete account), which removes your profile and data. Reply STOP to any text to stop receiving messages.
