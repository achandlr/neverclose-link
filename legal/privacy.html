Never Close: Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Never Close is a personal app that lets two people keep a running tab of who owes whom. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it. It describes what the app actually does; the in-app copy (Settings → Privacy Policy) says the same things in fewer words.

What we collect

The Apple or Google account you sign in with (and the email address it gives us), your display name, an optional avatar colour, and the expenses and tabs you create, including any receipt photo you attach.

Push token. If you allow notifications, a device identifier issued by Apple and Expo so we can deliver them. Notification text (a name and an entry's label or amount) passes through Expo's push service on its way to your phone.

Diagnostics. When something fails (a sign-in error, a failed save), the app records the error message, the build number, and your account id so we can fix it. Never the contents of your ledger.

Phone numbers. The app no longer asks for one. Accounts created with a phone number in an earlier version still have that number on file; it is never shared and is removed with your account.

We never collect financial or bank data, your home address, or government ID. Never Close records amounts; it does not move money.

Text messages (SMS)

We never send text messages. No verification codes, no invitations, none at all, so there is nothing to opt out of. You start a tab by sending someone an invite link through whatever app the two of you already use.

Your contacts

We never read them. The app requests no contacts access and does no phone-number matching, so your address book is never read, hashed, or uploaded, and we hold no record of your friends' numbers.

How we handle your data

Your tabs and expenses are visible only to you and the one other person on each tab, enforced at the database level. Never Close never moves money and never connects to your bank or cards. We don't sell your data or use your records for advertising.

Your choices

Delete your account anytime in the app (Settings → Delete account). This deletes your account, your name and profile details, your device tokens, your notifications, your blocks and your diagnostics, along with any tab nobody else is in and its receipt photos. Because a tab is shared, the other person keeps their own record of your shared history; your entries there remain, attributed to a deleted user. Anonymous diagnostic records may remain.
