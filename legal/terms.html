Never Close: Terms of Service

Last updated: September 2026

Never Close keeps a running tab of who owes whom, between two friends or across a group. It records amounts: it never moves money. There is no payment, cash-out, or bank connection.

Your account

You sign in with your Apple or Google account. No phone number is required. You're responsible for activity on your account and for the accuracy of what you record. You must be at least 13.

Text messages

We never send text messages. Never Close has no SMS of any kind: no verification codes, no invitations, nothing. You start a tab by sending someone an invite link through whatever app you two already use, so there is nothing to opt out of.

Ending your account

You can delete your account anytime in the app (Settings → Delete account). Deleting removes your account and your personal data. A tab you share stays with the people on it, who keep their own record of your shared history. We may suspend accounts that violate these terms.

Acceptable use

Don't use Never Close to harass, defraud, or record anything unlawful. Don't abuse, disrupt, or reverse-engineer the service.

Disclaimers

The service is provided "as is." Balances reflect only what the people on a tab enter: Never Close is not a party to your arrangement and isn't responsible for disputes between you. To the extent allowed by law, our liability is limited.
