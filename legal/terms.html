Never Close: Terms of Service

Last updated: July 2026

Never Close keeps a running tab of who owes whom between two people. It records amounts: it never moves money. There is no payment, cash-out, or bank connection.

Your account

Text messages

By providing your mobile number you consent to receive transactional texts (verification codes and invitations). Message frequency is occasional. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

Acceptable use

Don't use Never Close to harass, defraud, or record anything unlawful. Don't abuse, disrupt, or reverse-engineer the service.

Disclaimers

The service is provided "as is." Balances reflect only what you and the other person enter: Never Close is not a party to your arrangement and isn't responsible for disputes between you. To the extent allowed by law, our liability is limited.
